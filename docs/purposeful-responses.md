# RPG2 — Purposeful responses: working table and analysis

Saved October 12, 2026. Working proposal, not a finalized decision or biological taxonomy. Set aside for later discussion while examining targets.

Response structure: action + target + intended outcome. Distinguish immediate outcomes from ultimate benefits. Example: explore to locate shelter → approach shelter to reach it → enter shelter to reduce cold exposure.

## Analysis

One pressure can support many responses. Danger can produce hiding, freezing, withdrawing, attacking, or signaling. Disposition may influence the preferred response.

One action can serve many purposes. Searching for water, companions, or a mate requires different cues and success conditions. Distinct purposeful responses can reuse underlying capabilities.

The pressure column mixes bodily deficits, appraisals, caregiving, and anticipated conditions. Calling all of these needs would repeat the conceptual mixing we are trying to avoid.

| Pressure type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Bodily deficit or disturbance | Hunger, thirst, fatigue, sleep pressure, cold, heat, injury, irritation |
| Social deficit or disturbance | Separation, insufficient affiliative contact, crowding |
| Appraised threat | Danger to self, offspring, territory, or resources |
| Appetitive drive | Curiosity, play, mating |
| Anticipated condition | Future hunger, expected exposure, possible intrusion |

Derive needs from only part of this table. Panksepp motivations could influence responses across pressure types; disposition could influence preferred ways of responding. Do not force every purposeful response into a need.
